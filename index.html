Conditions we treat with care

Expert diagnosis and treatment for mental, neurological and behavioural conditions, with care for every age group.

Schizophrenia

Talking or laughing to oneself, hearing voices, unfounded fear or suspicion, suicidal thoughts and social withdrawal.

Depression

Persistent low mood, loss of interest in work, weakness and tiredness, feeling hopeless or helpless.

Learn More
03

Mania

Sleeplessness, leaving home without reason, excessive talking, over-excitement and impulsive spending.

Learn More
04

Anxiety

Constant worry and restlessness, sweating, rapid heartbeat, low self-confidence and fear of crowds or speaking.

Learn More
05

Panic Disorders

Sudden intense fear, chest pain or breathlessness, palpitations and a fear of dying.

Learn More
06

OCD

Repeating the same act or thought again and again, excessive cleanliness and washing, spending hours on one task.

Learn More
07

Migraine

Unbearable headache, heaviness in the head, and pain accompanied by vomiting or dizziness.

Learn More
08

Dementia

Memory loss in old age, forgetting names, addresses and faces, misplacing things and forgetting past events.

Learn More
09

Sleep Disorder

Trouble falling asleep, not getting enough sleep, late sleep-onset and repeated waking at night.

Learn More
10

Epilepsy

Tingling in hands, feet or body, tremors or stiffness, falling unconscious and foaming at the mouth.

Learn More
11

Sexual Disorders

Premature ejaculation, low sexual desire, semen-related complaints and erectile concerns.

Learn More
12

Hysteria

Repeated fainting spells, feeling possessed, loss of function in a body part and seizure-like jerks.

Learn More
13

Addiction

Alcohol, ganja, bhang, charas, opium or other intoxicants, with restlessness, fear, tremors or sleeplessness when withdrawn.

Learn More
14

Childhood Disorders

Intellectual disability, no interest in studies, anger, hyperactivity, fainting, substance use or cruelty to animals.
